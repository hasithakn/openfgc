Consent Purposes
create_custom_insurance_policy
Create Custom Insurance Policy
Mandatory
We collect your full name, email address, and age to design a personalised life insurance plan tailored to your specific needs. Your age enables us to calculate an accurate premium based on your risk profile. Your contact details ensure we can deliver your policy documents, send renewal notices, and reach you if we need to discuss your coverage. Your name is used to personalise your policy agreement.
marketing_via_email
Receive Marketing Communications via Email
Optional
We will send you personalised insurance offers, policy renewal reminders, and helpful tips on protecting your family — delivered directly to your inbox. You can unsubscribe at any time.
course_enrollment_and_certification
Course Enrolment & Certificate Issuance
Optional
Your name is printed on your certificate. Your date of birth is required to verify age under the DPDP Act. Your email is used for account access and course updates.
